## ✨ Features

- **Fully responsive design** – Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- **Clean & modern UI** – Focuses on readability and visual appeal.
- **Project showcase** – Highlights your best work with descriptions and links.
- **About section** – Shares your background, skills, and interests.
- **Contact integration** – Easy ways for recruiters or collaborators to reach you.
- **Optimized performance** – Lightweight code for fast loading.

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5** – Semantic markup
- **CSS3** – Custom styling with Flexbox/Grid (and media queries for responsiveness)
- **Font Awesome / Icons** – For professional iconography *(if used)*
- **Google Fonts** – For enhanced typography *(if used)*
